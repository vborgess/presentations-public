Trilinear wave interactions

Drag the frequencies or choose a preset. Compare the output with the paraboloid, and rotate the right view to inspect the geometry.

The output is (ξ+η, |ξ|²+|η|²). It lies on the paraboloid exactly when ξ·η = 0.

About this picture

This is a model with two frequency coordinates and one temporal frequency. The base plane is lowered for visual separation; the paraboloid remains τ = |ξ|². The vertical difference between the output and the surface is |ξ|²+|η|²−|ξ+η|² = −2ξ·η. Acute inputs give an output below the surface; obtuse inputs give an output above it. The resonant presets keep ξ fixed and shorten η along the vertical frequency axis.

All three frequencies stay within the displayed disk. Dragging the output preserves ξ−η. The inset shows unit normals in the directions (−2p,1) for p=ξ, η, ξ+η, translated to a common origin. These are the normals to the paraboloid at the corresponding frequencies, including when the output is off the surface. The inset’s position, orientation, and scale stay fixed. The main view zooms out when needed to keep a high output visible.
